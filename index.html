Lamb Shank with Fattah

Slow-cooked lamb shank served over traditional Egyptian fattah — layers of fragrant rice, toasted bread, and rich garlic tomato sauce. Fattah is one of Egypt’s most beloved celebratory dishes, often enjoyed during family gatherings and special occasions, offering a warm taste of Egyptian hospitality and heritage.

Mix Grilled with Khalta Rice

A classic Egyptian mixed grill featuring juicy kofta “meatball”, tender shish tawook “grilled chicken skewer”, kebab “grilled beef”, and grilled lamb chops, served with aromatic khalta rice. Grilled meats are a central part of Egyptian dining culture, bringing together bold Middle Eastern flavors and the spirit of sharing meals with family and friends.
